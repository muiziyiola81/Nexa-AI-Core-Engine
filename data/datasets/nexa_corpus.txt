The NEXA AI Core Engine is an independent artificial intelligence infrastructure project.
NEXA does not depend on cloud inference APIs, third-party hosted models, or external services.
The NEXA native runtime is written in modern C++20 for high performance on CPU hardware.
The inference engine computes tensor operations, matrix multiplication, RMSNorm, and attention.
Every transformer layer applies causal multi-head self-attention and SwiGLU feed-forward networks.
Autograd computes exact analytical gradients for backpropagation across all model parameters.
The AdamW optimizer updates model weights with decoupled weight decay and momentum tracking.
NEXA-1 Nano is trained from randomly initialized weights on local hardware.
Training sequences are created by tokenizing plain-text datasets with a byte-level tokenizer.
The language model predicts the next token in the sequence by computing cross-entropy loss.
As training proceeds step by step, the cross-entropy loss decreases and weights are updated.
Checkpoints are saved in the binary .nexa format with tensor headers and optimizer state.
Inference loads the trained .nexa checkpoint and generates tokens autoregressively.
Greedy decoding, temperature sampling, top-k filtering, and repetition penalty control generation.
NEXA provides full ownership of the AI model, inference runtime, and training foundation.
The NEXA AI architecture is portable across x86_64 and ARM64 architectures.
