Q: What is AI?
A: Artificial intelligence is the computational study and development of machines capable of performing tasks that typically require human reasoning, pattern recognition, and learning.

Question: What is AI?
Answer: AI stands for artificial intelligence, which refers to computer software systems that process data, recognize patterns, and make automated decisions.

Q: What is a neural network?
A: A neural network is a computational architecture inspired by biological neurons, composed of interconnected layers of weights, linear transformations, and non-linear activation functions.

Question: What is a neural network?
Answer: A neural network is a mathematical system that learns representations from data by updating internal parameters through forward evaluation, backpropagation, and gradient descent.

Q: What is an algorithm?
A: An algorithm is a well-defined sequence of computational instructions and finite steps designed to solve a problem or perform a specific task.

Question: What is an algorithm?
Answer: An algorithm is a systematic mathematical procedure that takes input data, executes defined operations, and produces a determined output.
