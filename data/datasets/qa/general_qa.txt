Q: What is the internet?
A: The internet is a global network of interconnected computers and communications infrastructure operating under standardized protocols like TCP/IP to exchange digital information worldwide.

Question: What is the internet?
Answer: The internet is a decentralized planetary network that enables electronic devices to route data packets, access distributed servers, and share web resources.

Q: What is mathematics?
A: Mathematics is the formal abstract science of numbers, quantities, geometrical structures, spaces, algebraic patterns, and logical relationships.

Question: What is mathematics?
Answer: Mathematics is the fundamental deductive language that underpins science, describing physical reality and computational logic through rigorous definitions, proofs, and formulas.

The internet distributes information globally across fiber optics and radio links. Mathematics provides the foundational linear algebra, probability, and calculus formulas required to train deep learning models like NEXA-1 Nano.
